Our mission

Give every SaaS team one calm source of truth.

We started Stackly after watching finance, support, and product teams reconcile the same numbers three different ways every week. That waste is what we set out to remove.

Our story

How Stackly came to be.

2021

Founded in a spare bedroom

Two former SaaS operators start building the billing tool they wished they'd had.

2022

First 100 customers

Word of mouth from early customers fuels the first wave of growth.

2023

Series A & analytics launch

Stackly Analytics ships, turning raw billing data into real-time insight.

2025

SOC 2 Type II certified

Enterprise customers join as security and compliance mature.

2026

12,000+ teams strong

Stackly becomes the system of record for a growing global customer base.
